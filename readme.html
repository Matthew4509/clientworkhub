Why use Client Work Hub

Most of running a service business is the same loop: take on a client, agree the work, do it on the right days, get paid, and keep the logins safe. This puts that loop on one screen.

Free, with nothing to renew

No subscription, no client limit, no licence key. Download it, install it, use it for as long as you like.

Your server, your data

It runs on your own hosting under your own domain. Nothing about your clients or your work is sent anywhere.

A vault for client logins

Website, social and hosting sign-ins are encrypted with a key kept outside the database, and every reveal is logged.

Your day, planned for you

Jobs turn into daily, weekly or monthly tasks that land on the days you work, with a timer to see where the hours went.

Invoices when you want them

Draft, issue and record payments, with your own tax and a printable copy your client can open from a link.

Installs in minutes

Plain PHP and MySQL. Upload one zip, open the installer, fill in one form. No command line, no build step.

What it does

Ninety per cent of using it is: add a client, add a job, do the work, know where the day went. Everything else is there when you want it and switched off until then.

Clients, with everything in one file

The person or business you work for, and everything that goes with them.

  • Add a client from any screen in a few short fields. Only a name is required.
  • Standing instructions, such as "articles of 500 words" or "never post before 9am", follow their work wherever it is done.
  • Contacts, properties, jobs, invoices and a history of every email and call, on tabs.
  • Mark a client VIP to move their work up the queue.

Jobs that plan themselves

A job is a piece of work with a price, a start and a length. It creates the tasks that fill your days.

  • Three steps: who it is for, what the work is, when it runs.
  • Leave the name or the date empty and it fills them in.
  • Paste the client's brief once; it shows on every task of that job.
  • See progress, value and status for every job in one list.

The task screen, where the work happens

Open a task and everything you need is there: who it is for, the job description, what was done last time, and the sign-in for the website or account you are working on.

  • Start, pause and finish a timer without leaving the page.
  • Copy the username and reveal the password with one deliberate click.
  • Write what you did in words the client can read, and keep private notes beside it.

Your whole workload in one list

The dashboard shows today. Workflow shows everything: every task by day, with Start, Mark as done and Edit beside each one.

  • Set your working days, hours and breaks, and tasks land only on days you work.
  • Number what matters most and the queue follows your order.
  • Reminders that repeat and snooze, and appointments that block out time.

Invoices and payments

Optional, and ready when you need it.

  • Draft first, then issue: the number is given at issue, so there are no gaps.
  • One tax of your own naming, added on top or included in the price.
  • Record payments in full or in part, and refunds beside them.
  • A printable invoice and the client's own copy at a link, from the same layout.

Know where the money is

Reports show a year of work at a glance, the value of what you run, and which clients pay best for the hours you put in. It is usually not the client you expected.

Services you can sell again

Save a piece of work you do often as a service, with its price, length and tasks. Start a job from it in two clicks, or publish it on an optional booking page.

Works on your phone

The screens rearrange themselves for a phone, so you can check the day or tick off a task away from your desk.

Updates you control

The site never checks the internet on its own. When a new release comes out, download it from GitHub and upload it on the Update screen. It is checked file by file, refused unless it carries the release signature (on servers whose PHP has the sodium extension, which most do), and it will not run until you have taken a backup.

Also included

A link that brings clients back

Every summary and follow-up can carry one private link per client, where they leave feedback, see their history and book again. No password, no account.

Email that stays polite

Four messages you can reword, merge codes, a record of everything sent, a send limit that suits shared hosting, and an unsubscribe that really stops everything.

Onboarding forms

Design the questions you need answered before work starts. Answers arrive on the client's file and any passwords go straight into the vault.

An optional booking page

Publish your services with a link for each, and take enquiries that you turn into clients and jobs yourself.

Backups and restore

One-click backups, plain or sealed with four words only you know, and a restore screen that reads the whole file before it changes anything.

Two-step sign-in and an audit log

Codes from an authenticator app with ten backup codes, rate limits on sign-in, and a log of every sensitive action that no screen can edit.

A health check

Answers from inside the site what a technician would check from a command line: PHP version, encryption, whether every file arrived intact, whether the database is current.

Demo mode and help

Fifty invented clients to look around with, removed in one click, and a Help button on every screen that explains the page you are on.

System requirements

Ordinary shared hosting is enough. The installer checks your server before it starts and writes a message you can send your host naming anything missing.

WhatNeededWhat it is for
PHP 8.1 or newerRequiredTested up to PHP 8.5.
MySQL 5.7+ or MariaDB 10.3+RequiredOne database and a user with all privileges on it.
pdo_mysql, mbstring, opensslRequiredThe database connection, text handling, and encryption of stored passwords.
HTTPSStrongly advisedIt holds real passwords and sends sign-in links.
zipOptionalInstalling updates from the Update screen.

Install in six steps

The same on any cPanel host. INSTALL.md and SETUP.md go into every detail.

  1. Download the release. Get clientworkhub-0.3.19.zip from the Releases page: https://github.com/Matthew4509/clientworkhub/releases
  2. Create a database. In cPanel, MySQL Databases: create a database and a user, and give the user all privileges on it.
  3. Upload and extract. In File Manager, upload the zip and extract it where the app should live, for example public_html/hub/. If the domain already has a website, use a folder or a subdomain.
  4. Run the installer straight away. Open yoursite.com/hub/install.php, enter the database details and your admin account. It creates the encryption key and deletes itself when it finishes.
  5. Turn on two-step sign-in. The last screen links to it. Keep the ten backup codes somewhere other than your phone.
  6. Add your business name. Settings, Invoicing, Your business, and the From name under Settings, Email. Then add your first client.
Updating later: download the newer zip from the Releases page and upload it on Settings, Update, exactly as it downloaded. Take the backup it asks for first.
